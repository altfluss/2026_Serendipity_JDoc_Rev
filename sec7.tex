\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper has asked how to build a representation space in which what
the descriptors cannot express can be sought. What it found is that the
boundary of denotation does not set the limit of seeking, but becomes
the foundation for passing beyond it: standing on that boundary, one is
given the direction to leap---straight up, orthogonal to everything the
descriptors account for. In this sense, denotation and connotation are
complementary, not at odds. Here, we see the shape of serendipity
itself---one searches every name one has, and then, from some
encounter, a dimension one had never had comes into view. This would be
the moment when denotation and connotation meet, and form an insight in
the seeker. That is what we see through this work.
