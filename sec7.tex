\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper has asked how to build a representation space in which one can
seek what the descriptors cannot express. It found that the boundary of
denotation becomes the foundation for passing beyond it, not the limit of
seeking: standing on that boundary, one finds the direction to
leap---straight up, orthogonal to everything the descriptors account for. In
this sense, denotation and connotation are complementary, not at odds. Here,
we see the shape of serendipity itself---one tries every name one has, and
then, from some encounter, a dimension one never had comes into view. This is
the moment when denotation and connotation meet, and form an insight in the
seeker. That is what this work lets us see.
